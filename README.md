# Upstate Voter Guide 2026

A plain-language, nonpartisan guide to South Carolina's Nov. 3, 2026 general election, focused on Anderson, Greenville, and Spartanburg counties.

- **Make a plan:** pick your county and how you'll vote; get dates, places, what to bring, and a calendar reminder.
- **See your ballot:** every candidate in ABC order, with a one-line description of what each office does.
- **Yes/No questions:** what a Yes or No vote actually does.

Not affiliated with any candidate, party, or campaign. Made by Lee Burgess ([@sgtkoala](https://instagram.com/sgtkoala)).

## Updating

All content lives in `data.js`. Edit it and push; the page rebuilds itself. Photos are shown in a race only when every candidate in that race has one.

Always confirm details at [scVOTES.gov](https://scvotes.gov).
